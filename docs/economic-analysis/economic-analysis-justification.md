# LaPrizza – Simple economic analysis

The figures come from `economic-analysis.xlsx`. The sources (F1–F3) are listed at the bottom of the sheet.

## 1. Business plan (Revenue – Costs – Expenses = Net result)

- **Revenue:** pizzas sold × net price per pizza. The price excludes VAT, because VAT belongs to the State (13% food, 23% drinks).
- **Costs:** ingredients and packaging, which grow with sales (30.5% of revenue).
- **Expenses:** costs that exist even without sales: staff, rent, marketing and other.
- **Depreciation:** the investment spread over the 5 years. It is not cash going out.
- **Tax:** 16.5% (15% SME rate + 1.5% municipal surcharge). There is no tax in a year with a loss.
- **Net result:** revenue – costs – expenses – depreciation – tax.

## 2. Indicators

- **Cash flow:** Year 0 is the investment (–152,485 EUR). In each later year it is the net result plus depreciation.
- **NPV (VPL):** the sum of all cash flows, discounted at 12.2%, because money today is worth more than money later. The result is **–74,729 EUR**, which is below 0, so the investment is rejected.
- **Payback:** the time until the cumulative cash flow becomes positive. The result is **more than 5 years**.

## 3. Simplifications

- Prices and costs stay the same every year (no inflation).
- All assets are written off over the 5 years, so nothing is left over at the end.
- The starting cash is not recovered at the end.

All three are prudent, so the result is slightly worse than in a detailed model.
